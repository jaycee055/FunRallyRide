FunRallyRide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 (accounts added)

What FunRallyRide does

FunRallyRide lets a group of people on a trip see each other on a shared map, talk on a live group voice channel, and send group chat messages. You log in with an email and password. There are no profile photos: each person is shown by a nickname, initials and a color.

What we collect and why

  • Your account: email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash by Google Firebase Authentication), used to log in, confirm your email and reset your password.
  • Your profile: nickname, chosen color and default vehicle type. There are no profile photos.
  • Your location (latitude, longitude, speed, direction and accuracy), only while a trip you joined is live and you have not paused sharing. It is used to show you on the trip map and to work out the group order and gaps.
  • Chat messages, join requests, alerts (SOS) and trip details (trip name, route and stops) that you or your group create.

Who can see it

Only members of the same trip who have been approved by the trip leader can see your location, chat and status. Anyone with a trip code can see that trip's name, leader nickname and planned route before asking to join. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Voice

The live mic sends your voice directly between the phones in your trip (peer-to-peer). Voice is not recorded or stored by FunRallyRide. To set up the connection, phones exchange technical connection details through the trip database and contact a free connection-helper (STUN) server run by Cloudflare or Google, which sees your phone's internet address.

Services FunRallyRide uses

  • Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Authentication): stores your account, profile and trip data, delivers it to trip members, and sends confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • OpenStreetMap and Esri: map and satellite images; place search (Photon by Komoot, and Nominatim) and route lines (OSRM) when you search places or set a route.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website version of the app.
  • cdnjs, jsDelivr and Google Fonts: deliver the map library, Firebase library and fonts.

These services receive your internet address and the requests your device makes to them, under their own privacy policies.

How long data is kept

When the leader ends a trip, live location data and voice connection details are deleted from the trip database. The trip name, route, member nicknames and chat stay in the database so the trip summary can be shown. A short trip history is also saved on your own phone. You can ask for a trip to be deleted (see Contact).

Your choices

  • You can pause location sharing at any time from the Members tab, or leave the trip.
  • You can mute your microphone at any time. The leader can mute the group channel in the app, but cannot control your phone's microphone outside FunRallyRide.
  • You can turn off location and microphone permissions in your phone's settings.
  • You can change your profile or password, or permanently delete your account, in the app under Settings.

Safety

GPS can be inaccurate, delayed or unavailable, and connections can drop. FunRallyRide does not contact emergency services; the SOS button only alerts your group. Do not operate your phone while driving or riding.

Children

FunRallyRide is not designed for children under 13. Children should only use it with a parent or guardian on the same trip.

Contact
